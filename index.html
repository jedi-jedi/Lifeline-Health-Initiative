Our Mission

A Compassionate and Resilient Society

Lifeline Health Initiatives Nigeria is dedicated to improving the survival, dignity, protection, health, and well-being of crisis-affected populations. With special attention to internally displaced persons, vulnerable women, sick women, and children, we provide timely humanitarian relief, medical support, and sustainable empowerment programs.

Our Programs

Comprehensive initiatives designed to restore dignity and build resilience.

🆘

Emergency Relief

Rapid response distribution of food, clean water, clothing, shelter materials, and hygiene supplies to crisis victims and displaced families.

🏥

Medical Outreach

Mobile clinics providing basic healthcare, prenatal care, safe delivery support, immunization awareness, and nutrition programs.

🛡️

Protection Programs

Safe spaces, psychosocial support, counseling, and case referral services for survivors of gender-based violence and child abuse.

🏠

IDP Assistance

Basic needs support, shelter assistance, and reintegration programs promoting dignity and self-reliance for displaced persons.

📚

Education Support

School supplies, learning materials, temporary learning spaces, and programs to reduce dropout rates among vulnerable children.

💼

Women's Empowerment

Vocational training, small business support, and income-generating activities for caregivers, widows, and female-headed households.
